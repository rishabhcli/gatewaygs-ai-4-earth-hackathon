# Simulation ownership

Owns physics-informed synthetic plume injection over immutable real artifact
backgrounds and records every sampled regime and seed.

It may consume validated retrieval-domain arrays but not held-out validation
events, UI state, provider SDKs, or training-framework globals. Its first
executable slice must version immutable train/tune/evaluation manifests and
prove deterministic replay for flux, wind, surface, turbulence, and cloud
regimes. Until then, simulation is explicitly unavailable.
